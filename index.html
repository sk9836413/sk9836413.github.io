Vatsin Workspace

An Indian HR, payroll and workplace suite: 17 products on one employee record, sold one at a time or as 10 industry bundles. Factories are the first market.

  • Indian payroll built in. PF with the ₹25,000 ceiling, ESI, PT, LWF, TDS under the Income-tax Act 2025, Labour Code registers, gratuity and full-and-final.
  • Made for the shop floor. Kiosk tablet with face or PIN punch, eSSL/ZKTeco biometrics, RFID access, gate passes, canteen wallets and piece-rate wages.
  • Mobile in 11 languages. Android apps per product in 10 Indian languages plus English, built with React Native and Expo.
  • AI on its own server. A self-hosted Qwen assistant that scores résumés, reads receipts, explains payroll changes and drafts help-desk replies.
  • Connects to what companies use. REST API, webhooks, SCIM, SSO/SAML, passkeys, WhatsApp, Slack and Teams bots, and an ERP framework starting with Business Central.
  • Multi-tenant from day one. Automatic tenant scoping on every query, tenant-to-database routing, read replicas, point-in-time backups and a DR runbook.
  • Planned for scale. Stateless instances with Redis-backed state, leader locks for jobs, k6 load tests and a capacity plan from 1,000 to 10 crore users.
  • Secure by design. 2FA, passkeys, field-level encryption of PII, Azure Key Vault, CSP, a SIEM feed and a NOC/SOC portal with a CERT-In reporting helper.

Procure-to-pay workflow portal

Takes every purchase across several plants from request to payment. Each step is a task with an owner, a deadline, a reminder and an escalation.

  1. Request
  2. Approval
  3. RFQ and quotes
  4. Purchase order
  5. Dispatch
  6. Gate entry
  7. Receipt and QC
  8. Invoice check
  9. Payment
  • Approval levels by value, budget and plant. HOD steps go to the requester’s manager; self-approval is blocked.
  • Indian tax handled by the system. GSTIN checksum, HSN/SAC, CGST+SGST or IGST by state, TDS sections and e-invoice IRN checks.
  • Budgets that can’t double-post. A ledger commits spend on PO approval and records actuals on payment, with idempotent postings.
  • One-screen approvals. Amount with tax, budget impact, last purchase price, quotes and the approval chain, with Approve, Send back and Reject.
  • Imports, rate contracts, service POs and emergency POs, each with its own rules, plus quality holds and three-way invoice matching.
  • Tested against a real database. 17 end-to-end suites with 379 checks, a role-access audit and a 25-page UI audit on desktop, phone and dark mode.

Sales, CRM and licence compliance portal

Sales, receivables and licences in one portal, with live Business Central data. Taken from prototype to production.

  • The portal can stop an invoice. A Business Central extension checks the portal before an invoice posts. It is blocked, allowed or sent to a maker-checker approval queue with amount limits.
  • Rules without code. Licence rules live in a master screen the business edits itself.
  • Incremental sync of 12 entities through custom AL API pages every 15 minutes or on demand, with export invoices converted to INR and balances rebuilt from the ledger.
  • 8 roles with row-level security checked on every server action and API route, plus Customer 360 and dashboards for targets, pipeline, receivables ageing and market share.
